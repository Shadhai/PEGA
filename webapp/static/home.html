How it works

Every plan passes through the same four gates.

The full pipeline — classifier, plan libraries, graph validation and bounded recovery — is laid out in detail on the systems page.

01

Classify

A local, 4-bit Qwen-2.5-1.5B model routes the command to one of five plan libraries.

02

Search

Adaptive coordinate search proposes a candidate path before anything touches hardware.

03

Validate

Every step is checked against the KùzuDB constraint graph via Cypher — not a heuristic.

04

Decide & Log

Approve, recover, or reject — with a cited evidence trail either way.

PROBLEM

LLMs hallucinate plans that sound right and physically can't happen.

In safety-critical domains — space, disaster response, industrial arms — a confidently wrong plan is worse than a slow one. PAGA exists to catch action hallucination before execution, using physics and a constraint graph instead of another model's confidence score.
